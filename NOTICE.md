# Research and third-party materials

Research materials in the source are attributed to Fatemeh Alizadeh. No blanket open-source license has been assigned by this packaging work. The author can select a license for materials they control before public release.

SciER data, pretrained model weights, model adapters derived from those weights, and software dependencies retain their own terms. Download data and models through their official sources and comply with their licenses and gated-access requirements. This repository excludes full datasets, weight files, optimizer states, and presentation artwork with unspecified redistribution terms.

The original report is retained as supplied; code-audit qualifications about test threshold selection and missing artifacts are documented in `docs/methods.md`.
